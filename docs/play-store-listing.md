# Google Play Listing Draft

## App name

ScrollBill

## Short description

See your week in screen time, then share the receipt.

## Full description

ScrollBill turns your Android app-usage history into a clear weekly screen-time report.

See your total app time, daily average, yearly pace, and the applications that took the most time. When you are ready, create a clean weekly phone receipt and share it through Android's standard Sharesheet.

ScrollBill processes the report on your device. There is no account, advertising, analytics, cloud upload, or automatic publishing. Usage Access is a special Android setting that you control, and sharing happens only when you choose a destination.

Current reports cover the last seven completed local calendar days. The yearly pace is an approximate projection based on that week's daily average, not a prediction or health assessment.

## Feature bullets

- Weekly screen-time total and daily average
- Ranked view of your top applications
- Approximate yearly pace based on the weekly average
- Shareable 1080×1920 weekly phone receipt
- On-device processing with no account or backend

## Privacy statement

Your usage data stays on this device. ScrollBill does not upload your usage history.
